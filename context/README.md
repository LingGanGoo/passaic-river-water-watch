# context/ — the drop folder

This is the feed-it-back loop from the Water Watch roadmap.

**Your job each week:** photograph the field data sheet (or type the numbers
into a message) and drop it in here. That is the entire job. No transcribing,
no spreadsheets.

**What comes back** (ask the co-pilot to process the drop): the computed
CFU/100 mL and band, the published dataset row (JSON + CSV), the rebuilt
site, and a drafted Friday advisory for you to approve, edit, and send from
your own address.

Also drop here:
- lab split results when they come back (weeks 2, 5, 8) — they feed the
  quality assurance comparison
- notes from any protocol reviewer meeting — they feed corrections to the
  methods page

Field sheet fields, in the order you fill them: week number; station
(NBC-01); clock time on the dock; tide stage; 48 h antecedent rainfall from
your own gauge, in inches; water temperature; pH; turbidity; nitrate as N;
orthophosphate; plate A count; plate B count; incubation in time; incubation
out time; measured incubator temperature; notes.

Honesty rules that govern every row: exact counts, not estimates
("uncountable / TNTC" is a valid value; a guessed number is not); real clock
times; gaps recorded, never backfilled; incubator drift reported, not
corrected.
